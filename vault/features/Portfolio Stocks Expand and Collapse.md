# Portfolio Stocks Expand and Collapse

Status: shipped
Summary: Tapping the Stocks class row shows or hides its holdings inline.

## Details
`src/app/editorial/(tabs)/portfolio.tsx`: expanded by default, showing Apple, Microsoft, Vanguard Total and "8 more holdings". While expanded, the non-stock segments of the allocation bar dim to 32% and the Stocks monogram is selected. The Apple row opens Holding detail. The other class rows are static.
