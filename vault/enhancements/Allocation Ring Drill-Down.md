# Allocation Ring Drill-Down

Status: planned
Summary: Direct manipulation of the allocation ring: touch arcs, drill from class into holdings, and swap target/actual rings.

## Notes
Design Document §4.2: touch-down on an arc thickens it; dragging round the ring scrubs across arcs with haptic ticks; releasing drills down (the selected arc rotates to 12 o'clock, the others collapse to a 1px outer ring, the class unfolds into its holdings, the caption gains "· Stocks"); pinch-in or back reverses. A two-finger rotate swaps the actual and target rings. Today only legend-row selection exists.
