Patina

A quiet-luxury, editorial interface for watching wealth age well.

1 · Design concept

Name · Mood · Rationale

Patina. Bronze does not flash; it deepens. The app reads like the catalogue of a private collection: warm charcoal grounds instead of navy, bone-white type set in a serif with real optical sizes, one brass hairline where a frame edge would be, and captions in small tracked capitals under every number, as a museum labels an object. Gains oxidize to verdigris, losses to copper — the two colours bronze actually turns.

It fits money because trust is quiet. A user's net worth is the slowest-moving number in their life; a screen that flickers, pulses and shouts percentages contradicts that fact and manufactures anxiety. Patina treats each holding as an object to be looked at with distance and care — nothing urges, nothing sells — which is exactly the posture of calm confidence a long-horizon investor wants mirrored back.

2.3 Spacing · radius · surfaces

Space: 4-pt base — 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Screen gutter 24 (not 16: the margin is the brand). Section gap 48. Row height 64. Plinth padding 20. Radius: 0 rules · 6 chips/inputs · 14 plinths · 28 bottom sheets · full avatars. Surfaces: default containers are plinths — no fill, a 1px Hairline top rule, a Caption label, content. Surface-1 fill only for the tab bar and list separators; Surface-2 only for sheets, inputs and pressed states. Shadows: none. Elevation is expressed by lightening the ground, never by drop shadow.

2.4 Chart colour language

Asset classes are mineral, not spectral: Stocks Bone #ECE8DF · Crypto Brass #C9A961 · Real estate Umber #8C6A4F · Cash Ash #A8A499 · Savings Verdigris-dim #5F8A7E. These five never carry meaning of gain or loss. The time-series line is Bone, 1.5px, round joins; the area beneath is a 6%→0% Bone gradient. Only the selected-period delta chip and the sparkline of a single holding take Verdigris/Copper. No gridlines; one baseline hairline; at most three y-labels in Caption style; x-labels only at period boundaries. Selected series → full Bone, others fade to 32% (not recoloured).

2.5 Iconography

24-grid, 1.5px stroke, round caps, no fills — exhibition-wayfinding glyphs (Phosphor Thin or Lucide at 1.5 as the base set). Asset classes are not pictured; they are lettered: monograms S · C · RE · $ · SV in 28px Newsreader 400 inside a 1px Slate ring (brass ring when selected). Tab bar: four glyphs, no labels at default type size (labels appear at ≥ Large dynamic type). No emoji, no 3D, no illustration.

2.6 Motion

Two curves only. Settle cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), 320–480ms, for entrances and layout; Drift cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1), 160–220ms, for state changes. Gesture-driven motion uses a critically damped spring (ratio 1.0, stiffness 180) so nothing overshoots. Numbers never spin; digits cross-fade with a 24px vertical drift (§5). Staggers are 40ms, never more than six items. Opacity enters from 0 with a 12px rise, never from a scale below 0.96. Reduce Motion: durations 0, cross-fades only, chart draws appear complete.

4 · Hero interactions

Choreography with timings · all on Settle unless stated

4.1 The reveal — “developing the print.” 0 ms: Ink screen, caption “Net worth” already present at 40% opacity. 0–240 ms: the brass hairline draws left→right (right→left in AR) under the caption. 120 ms: the hero figure appears fully formed but at 0 opacity and blurred 12px, like a photograph in developer; opacity and blur resolve to 1/0 over 480 ms while the number rises 12px. Digits do not count up — counting-up performs a lottery, and this is not one. 400 ms: the fractional cents fade in at Slate. 480 ms: the two delta lines enter with 40 ms stagger, glyph first then number. 640 ms: the sparkline draws along its path over 560 ms with the area gradient trailing 80 ms behind. 900 ms: plinth bars grow from zero width in class order, 40 ms stagger. Total 1.3 s; the whole sequence is skipped after a warm resume < 5 min, where only the hairline re-draws to confirm freshness. With privacy blur on, the figure resolves to “••••••” and a long-press completes the development.

4.2 Allocation ring — “turning the object.” The ring is five arcs, 4px stroke, 8px gaps, on a 240px circle; the centre shows “All · $1.28M”. Touch-down on an arc: that arc thickens to 10px inward over 160 ms (Drift) and the others fall to 32% opacity; the centre label cross-fades to the class name and weight; the legend row for that class lifts its hairline to Brass. Drag around the ring scrubs across arcs with haptic ticks at each gap. Release on an arc drills down: the ring rotates so the selected arc's start sits at 12 o'clock (Settle, 400 ms), the other arcs shrink to a single 1px Slate outer ring, and the selected arc unfolds into that class's holdings — sub-arcs animate out from the parent arc's angle. Breadcrumb caption gains “· Stocks”. Pinch-in or back gesture reverses the fold. A two-finger rotate on the ring toggles actual/target rings swapping places — target becomes solid, actual dashed — so drift is read as a change of position rather than a warning colour.

4.3 Holding chart — “reading the plate.” Line in Bone 1.5px, full-bleed. Touch-down anywhere: a vertical 1px Slate rule appears under the finger with a 6px Bone dot on the line; the header hero cross-fades (digit drift, §5) to the value at that date and the caption to the date in Newsreader italic; the Δ pair re-reads relative to that point. Drag: rule and dot follow at 60/120 fps; haptic tick at month boundaries. Second finger down: a second rule appears, the region between them tints at 8% (Verdigris if end > start, Copper otherwise) and the header now reads the span (“+$2,410 · 4.1% · 14 Mar – 2 Jun”). Long-press on the line drops a permanent brass tick with a note field (“bought the dip”) that persists as a hairline marker. Period change (1M→1Y): the existing line morphs to the new path over 400 ms — points interpolate, never redraw from zero — while the y-axis labels cross-fade. Release: rules fade 200 ms, header settles back to now.

4.4 Add a holding — “the accession sheet.” Plus in the tab bar lifts a Surface-2 sheet to 92% height (Settle 400 ms). Step 1 asks one question in Newsreader 24: “What kind of holding?” with five monograms in a row; tap one and it slides to the top-left, the question collapses into “A stock —”. Step 2: the search field appears under it, keyboard up; typing “AAP” streams results as text rows with ticker in mono; choosing one appends “Apple, AAPL —” to the sentence. Step 3: quantity and price as two tnum fields side-by-side; a live preview line beneath reads “That's $12,410 · 0.97% of net worth” and updates on every keystroke with digit drift. Step 4: acquired date, defaulting to today, shown as prose (“acquired today”), tap to edit. The sentence at the top is now the confirmation: “A stock — Apple, AAPL — 50 at $248.20 — acquired today.” Every fragment is tappable to jump back. Brass “Add to collection” sits above the keyboard. On confirm: the sheet drops, the Home hero performs a digit drift to its new value, and the new row in Portfolio enters with its monogram ring in brass for 2 s before settling to Slate.

Number animation — “digit drift.” When a value changes, each digit that differs cross-fades to its new glyph while the old one drifts 24px in the direction of the change (up for increase, down for decrease) and the new one drifts in from the opposite side; 220 ms, Drift curve, all digits simultaneous. Unchanged digits do not move. Width is stable because everything is tnum. Magnitude changes (e.g. $999k → $1.0M) re-layout with Settle 320 ms. No odometer spinning, no count-up.

Calm decline. A loss is a fact, not an alarm. Copper is a warm mid-tone, not a red; it is used only on the delta glyph and number, never on a background, never on the hero figure, never in a badge. The glyph is a small filled triangle (▼) and the number uses a true minus; the caption reads “down 0.24% today” in prose. Charts under water keep the Bone line — only the period chip tints. Copy never uses “plunge”, “crash”, “loss” as a header; insights phrase declines as context (“Stocks are 3.1% below their 30-day mean; Crypto moved 62% of today's change”). Haptics on negative changes are identical to positive. No red dots, no shaking, no push notification for market moves under a user-set threshold (default: none).

6 · RTL / Arabic handling

Mirror · don't mirror · typographic deltas
ElementMirrorsRule
Layout, reading order, list alignment, back gesture, breadcrumbYesUse logical properties everywhere (start/end, marginStart). Captions sit top-right; values align left in AR. Hairline reveal draws right→left.
Time-series charts, x-axisYesTime flows right→left in AR (research with Arabic finance users and Gulf bank apps supports mirrored time). The “now” point lands at the start edge (right). Scrub gestures follow the finger regardless.
Allocation ring rotation, progress barsYesRing fills counter-clockwise from 12 o'clock; drill-down rotates the selected arc to 12 the same way. Progress fills from the right.
Numbers, currency codes, tickers, ▲▼ glyphs, minus signNoDigits are always LTR within the bidi run; wrap every figure in an isolate (U+2066…U+2069 / unicode-bidi: isolate). Currency symbol follows locale: “١٢٫٥٠٠ ر.س” vs “SAR 12,500” — never hardcode position. Tickers stay Latin mono.
Media/playback-style icons, clocks, brand monogramsNoDirectional navigation glyphs (chevron, back) mirror; physical-metaphor glyphs do not. Monogram letters become Arabic initials (أ · ع · ع.ق · ن · م) in Naskh, not transliterations.

Numerals. Default to Western Arabic digits (0–9) in all locales — they dominate Gulf finance — with a Settings toggle for Eastern Arabic-Indic (٠–٩). When Eastern digits are on, decimal is U+066B (٫) and thousands U+066C (٬); tabular-nums still applies (Plex Sans Arabic and Naskh both carry tnum). Percent sign follows the number with Arabic percent U+066A.

Typographic deltas. Arabic has no uppercase, so museum captions become Plex Sans Arabic 500 at 12px (not 11) with +0.04em tracking and a leading brass en-dash. Naskh display sizes run ~8% larger and line-height 1.25 instead of 1.05 for ascender/descender room: Hero 60/72, Title 26/34. Letter-spacing on Arabic running text is 0 (kashida joins break with tracking). Italic “as of” lines become Naskh 400 in Slate — no oblique. Mixed-script rows (Arabic name + Latin ticker) set the ticker in an LTR isolate with 8px gap. Line lengths: Arabic body wraps ~10% shorter; plinth captions may take two lines — reserve height for it rather than truncating.

7 · Accessibility

Contrast · non-colour encoding · targets · dynamic type

Contrast. All body and data text ≥ 4.5:1 (Bone 15.9, Ash 8.1, Slate 4.6 on Ink; Slate is limited to Caption and above 11px 500 weight). Hero and Display ≥ 3:1 trivially. Brass on Ink 8.6:1; Bone on Brass button 1.9:1 — so the primary button uses Ink text on Brass (8.6:1). Verdigris 8.3:1, Copper 6.6:1 on Ink; in light mode gain #3D7A69 (4.9:1) and loss #A85E3E (4.6:1) on Paper. Hairlines are decorative and exempt; focus rings are 2px Brass at 3:1+ against every surface.

Gain/loss beyond colour. Every delta carries three redundant channels: glyph (▲ / ▼ / —), sign (+ / true minus / none) and prose in the caption (“up”, “down”, “unchanged”). Verdigris and Copper were chosen to separate on luminance (L* 70 vs 63) and on the blue–yellow axis so deuteranopes and protanopes still see two hues. Sparklines add a 1px dotted baseline when below start. Calendar-grid squares print their value. Screen readers get “up 0.98 percent, 12,410 dollars over 30 days”, never “▲”.

Touch. Minimum 44×44 pt targets; list rows 64; ring arcs get an invisible 24px hit band on both sides of the stroke; chart scrub activates on touch-down anywhere in the chart's 240px band, not on the 1.5px line. Tab-bar glyphs without labels have accessibilityLabel; long-press shows the label as a tooltip.

Dynamic type. Body, Secondary, Caption and Data scale 1:1 with the system setting up to AX5; Title and Heading scale to 1.6×; Hero and Display cap at 1.3× and then abbreviate ($1.28M) rather than wrap. At ≥ Large, tab-bar labels appear, plinth captions move above values (no side-by-side), and the Provenance list becomes single-column. Reduce Motion honoured per §2.6; Reduce Transparency removes the 6% chart gradient; Bold Text maps 400→500 and 300→400. Increase Contrast swaps Slate for Ash and Hairline for #3A3D37.
